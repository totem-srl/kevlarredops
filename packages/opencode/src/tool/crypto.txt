Local cryptographic and encoding utilities — no network, no secrets leave the process.

Operations:
- hash: sha256/sha1/md5/sha512 of input.
- hmac: keyed hash (requires key).
- encode / decode: base64, base64url, hex, url, rot13.
- jwt_decode: split a JWT into header/payload/signature WITHOUT verifying.
- xor: repeating-key XOR.

Use for token inspection, payload crafting, hash comparison during testing.
