Records observations during an engagement: vulnerabilities, code smells, intel facts, flags, IOCs, control gaps, risks. Observations are append-only event-sourced records that feed remediation and reporting.

Actions:
- list: show all observations with severity counts.
- add: record a new observation (subtype/title required).
- update: patch title/severity/confidence/status/note/tags/subtype by id.
- remove: delete an observation by id.
- link_evidence: attach an evidence artifact sha256 to an observation.
