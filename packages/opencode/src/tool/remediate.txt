Turn a recorded observation into actionable remediation.

Two modes:
- advice: generic, standard-referenced remediation steps (OWASP/CWE/NIST) for the finding.
- patch: scaffold a minimal unified diff against the referenced file:line from the
  observation's evidence or notes, inserting a clearly marked TODO the calling agent must
  replace with a real fix. Never auto-applied.

Patch mode refuses deliberately vulnerable training fixtures (juice-shop, webgoat, dvwa,
bwapp) and falls back to advice. The produced payload is stored as evidence in the
engagement.
