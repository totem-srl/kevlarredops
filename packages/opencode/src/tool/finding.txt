Manage the finding lifecycle: raw observations become candidates, candidates get verified
against evidence and replay material, and only verified findings with evidence plus a replay
(or a structured, justified replay exemption) are reportable.

Actions:
- list: active reportable findings, resolved retests, a verification queue with blocking reasons, and excluded
  lifecycle records. Rechecks scope and evidence integrity using the report's snapshot builder.
- status: redacted revision history for one finding id
- promote: record operator verification with an affected target authorized by the current scope,
  resolving evidence refs and either replay material or a justified exemption. New findings require
  target. References are canonicalized to SHA-256; prefixes must be at least 8 characters and
  labels must be unambiguous. Missing or altered artifacts cannot be promoted. Repeated promotion
  preserves prior replay unless a replacement is supplied. This tool records verification; it
  does not independently execute or judge the submitted replay material.
- reject: mark a candidate rejected with a note
- update: assign owner and record impact, remediation and ordered reproduction_steps.
  Requires reviewer and a nonempty note; appends a revision without changing verification.
- retest: record resolved, still_vulnerable or inconclusive with reviewer, note and new
  stored proof plus replay or exemption. Proof must contain distinct bytes captured after
  the previous revision; include the retest timestamp and result. Original proof is kept.
  Resolved findings are separate from active vulnerabilities. Still_vulnerable reopens
  a resolved finding; inconclusive moves it to the verification queue. Absence from a
  scan does not establish resolution. Reviewer labels are operator assertions.

Use this at report time: `list` tells you exactly which findings still block the report.
