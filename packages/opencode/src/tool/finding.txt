Manage the finding lifecycle: raw observations become candidates, candidates get verified
against evidence and replay material, and only verified findings with evidence plus a replay
(or a structured, justified replay exemption) are reportable.

Actions:
- list: bucketed summary of all findings (reportable / unverified / no evidence / no replay / rejected / stale)
- status: detail for one finding id
- promote: verify a candidate by attaching evidence refs and either replay material or an exemption
- reject: mark a candidate rejected with a note

Use this at report time: `list` tells you exactly which findings still block the report.
