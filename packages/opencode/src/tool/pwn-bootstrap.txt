Bootstrap an engagement for a target in one step.

Classifies the target shape (URL, IP/CIDR, or domain), creates a new engagement named after
it, sets the target as the engagement scope, moves the phase to recon, and recommends the
matching starting play:

- URL → web-surface play
- IP/CIDR → network-surface play
- domain → osint-target play

Use this at the start of a session instead of manually initializing scope and phase.
