Manage the engagement evidence store.

Every probe, scan, and verification step should leave evidence here. Content is stored
content-addressed (sha256), deduplicated, and addressable by hash, prefix, or label.

Actions:
- add_text / add_file: store content with a label and source
- list: recent entries with hashes and labels
- search: filter entries by query (matches label, source, mime, hash, or text content)
- get: fetch one entry's metadata plus a text preview

Reference entries elsewhere (findings, observations) by sha256 or label.
