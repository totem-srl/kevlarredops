Raw network interaction for services that plain HTTP tools cannot reach.

Operations:
- banner_grab: connect to host:port and capture the service banner.
- tcp_send: connect, send a payload (utf8/hex/base64), read the response.
- udp_send: send a datagram and wait for one reply.

Hosts are checked against engagement scope before connecting. Output is byte-formatted with printable text plus \xNN escapes.
