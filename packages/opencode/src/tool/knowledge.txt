Look up vulnerability intelligence and knowledge-broker capabilities.

Two actions:
- vuln_intel: fetch a full intel card for a CVE id from the public NVD database — summary,
  CVSS severity, affected CPE ranges, applicability against an optional component/version,
  references, and safe next actions. Prefer this over `cve` when you have a single known
  CVE id and want the full formatted card.
- capabilities: list the knowledge intents, actions, and modes this broker supports.
