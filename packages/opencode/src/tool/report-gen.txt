Generate the engagement report from current state via report_gen. The builder is deterministic — it reads EngagementStore directly; do not restate findings from memory that are not in state.

When to call: at the reporting phase, when the operator requests a report (/report), or when an objective completes and its evidence should be captured.

Parameters:
- format: "markdown" (default), "json" (versioned structured report, not a state backup), or "html" (offline, printable). All use the same reporting model.
- sections: subset of executive_summary, objectives, scope, findings, attack_path, credentials, recommendations. Applies to every format. Default: all.
- output_path: write to file instead of returning inline.

Evidence rule: reportable findings come from the finding lifecycle, require verified status, an authorized recorded target, stored evidence whose bytes match SHA-256, and replay material or a reasoned exemption. Scanner observations remain in a verification queue. Rejected and stale findings do not become remediation recommendations. Missing, changed or ambiguous evidence blocks reportability; corrupt ledgers fail visibly.

Credential values and known credentials appearing in text are redacted. Raw evidence is not embedded. Every export includes review/coverage limits and a file digest in tool metadata. A matching hash verifies bytes, not the vulnerability itself; this exporter does not independently rerun replay material. Inspect other sensitive data before sharing.

Resolved findings appear in a separate group and do not increase active severity counts or remediation recommendations. They require a recorded retest with a reviewer, note, current proof and replay or exemption. Original evidence and revision history are retained and audited. An inconclusive retest stays in the verification queue. Absence from a later scan does not prove resolution.
