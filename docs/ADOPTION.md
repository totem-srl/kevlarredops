## How to use these figures

projects depend on KevlarRedOps. A maintainer declaration, commit history, stars, or forks
cannot replace an adoption metric requested by a verifier. Upstream downloads and dependents
must not be attributed to this derivative.

When independently verifiable adoption becomes available, update this page with the exact
repository or package identifier, metric, time window, date checked, and public source URL.
